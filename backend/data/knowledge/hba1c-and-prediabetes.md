# HbA1c and prediabetes

HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin) reflects **average blood glucose over roughly the past 2–3 months**. It is reported as a percentage.

## Thresholds

- **< 5.7%** — normal.
- **5.7–6.4%** — prediabetes range.
- **≥ 6.5%** — diabetes range.

## Interpretation notes

- The platform flags **5.7%** as the prediabetes threshold. Crossing it in a reading is recorded as a threshold crossing.
- A rise from ~5.2% to ~6.4% over a year is a meaningful upward drift and warrants attention, especially if the trajectory is still climbing.
- Small month-to-month changes (e.g. +0.4%) can still be clinically significant when repeated — look at the overall direction, not a single step.

## Interpretation with context

- Family history of type 2 diabetes raises the significance of an HbA1c trend.
- Like all biomarkers, interpret the trajectory, not the snapshot.

# HDL cholesterol

HDL cholesterol ("high-density lipoprotein") is often called the "good" cholesterol because of its association with lower cardiovascular risk.

## Units and ranges

- Measured in **mg/dL**.
- **≥ 60 mg/dL** — considered protective.
- **40–59 mg/dL** — acceptable for most adults.
- **< 40 mg/dL** — low; a risk factor, especially in the presence of other lipid abnormalities.

## Interpretation notes

- The platform flags **40 mg/dL** as the low threshold for HDL.
- A slowly falling HDL alongside a rising LDL is a compounding pattern — both move risk in the same direction.
- HDL alone is rarely a treatment target, but it is informative as part of the full lipid panel and trend.

## Clinical practice guidance

- Weight HDL changes within the full lipid context (LDL trajectory, genetic risk) rather than in isolation.
